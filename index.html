The right caregiver matters.

Home care isn't only about completing a list of tasks. It's about who is walking through the door.

Your loved one may spend many hours each week with their caregiver. That's why we take the time to understand their personality, routines, preferences, interests and culture, then build a consistent care team around them. Trust grows through familiarity.

We are more than respite care.

We support the person receiving care and the family who loves them.

Why families choose United Respite Care

Consistent, familiar caregivers
We build a dedicated care team and focus on continuity, rather than introducing someone new every week.
Thoughtful caregiver matching
Matching considers care needs as well as personality, interests and cultural fit.
Families stay in the loop
We're hands-on and relationship-driven. Families aren't simply assigned a caregiver and left on their own.
Care that adapts
We stay involved and adjust the care plan as needs change.
Our caregiver standards

Our care team is carefully selected and screened. Caregivers have extensive care experience or recognized home-care training, and team members meet the professional, screening and safety requirements of their roles. All are covered under WCB and liability insurance.
